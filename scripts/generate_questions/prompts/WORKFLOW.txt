We're building a database of "buckets". Each bucket contains a pool of citizen questions and a pool of imposter questions that all share an answer format, so any citizen question can be randomly paired with any imposter question to produce a valid round.

Our workflow for building each bucket:

1. I'll first send you a message describing in detail what makes a good question versus a bad one for this game, with examples. Read it carefully — every question you generate must follow those principles.
2. Then I'll ask you to generate candidate question pairs on any topic of your choosing. Each pair is one citizen question and one imposter question that share an answer format. I'll review the pairs and pick one I like by sending you its index starting from 1.
3. Once I pick a winning pair, I'll ask you to expand it into a full bucket: 10 citizen questions and 10 imposter questions, all anchored around the same answer format as the chosen pair. I'll review these and tell you which ones make it into the database.
4. After the bucket is finalized, we end the conversation and start a new one for the next bucket. So everything you generate in this conversation belongs to one bucket, built around one anchor pair.

A few standing rules for everything you generate:

- I will sometimes paste a list of question topics already used in previous buckets. Do not reproduce or closely paraphrase any of them. Aim for genuine novelty, not minor rewording.
- Within any list of candidates you produce, each question must be meaningfully different from the others — not the same question rephrased.
- Wait for my instructions at each step. Don't skip ahead. Don't generate the bucket before I pick a pair. Don't pick a pair for me.
